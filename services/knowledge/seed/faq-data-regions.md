title: "Which regions can I store data in, and can I move it later?"
tags: regions, data residency, eu-west, us-east, ap-south, location
---
Helix Cloud runs in three regions: eu-west (Dublin), us-east (Virginia) and ap-south (Mumbai).
You choose the region when you create a project. Data cannot be moved to another region afterwards;
to change region, create a new project and copy the data across.
