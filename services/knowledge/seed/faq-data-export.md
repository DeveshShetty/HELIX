type: faq
title: "How do I export all of my organisation's data?"
tags: data export, gdpr, archive, download all data, portability
---
An owner can request a full export under Settings > Data > Export archive. We prepare a ZIP of all
projects, users and audit logs and email a download link within 24 hours. The link expires after 7 days.
