title: "What is the uptime SLA?"
tags: sla, uptime, availability, credits, guarantee
---
Team and Enterprise plans include a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If we miss it, you can claim service
credits of 10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% below target, up to 50%, within 30 days.
