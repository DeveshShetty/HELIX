title: "How do I reset my password?"
tags: login, password, reset, forgot password, account
---
Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We send a reset link
that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.
If your organisation uses single sign-on, reset your password with your identity provider instead.
