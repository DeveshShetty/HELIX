title: "Notification emails are delayed or not arriving"
tags: email, notifications, delayed, spam, not receiving
---
Symptoms: alert or notification emails from Helix Cloud arrive late or not at all.

Steps:
1. Check the spam folder and add notifications@helix.example to your allow list.
2. Check Settings > Notifications that the email channel is enabled for that alert type.
3. Corporate mail filters sometimes quarantine automated mail; ask your IT team to release it.
4. As an alternative, enable Slack or webhook notifications on the same page.
