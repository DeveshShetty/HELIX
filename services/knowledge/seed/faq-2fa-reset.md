title: How do I reset two-factor authentication (2FA)?
tags: login, 2fa, mfa, authenticator, account
---
If you lost access to your authenticator app, sign in with one of the 10
backup codes you saved when enabling 2FA, then go to Settings > Security >
Two-factor authentication > Reset.

If you have no backup codes, an organisation admin can reset 2FA for you from
Admin > Users > (user) > Reset 2FA. Helix Cloud support cannot reset 2FA on
your behalf for security reasons.
